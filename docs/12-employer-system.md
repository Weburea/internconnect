# Employer System

## Purpose

Allows companies and recruiters to post internships, review candidates, manage interviews, and hire talent.

---

## Employer Dashboard

Widgets

- Active Internship Posts
- Total Applications
- Shortlisted Candidates
- Scheduled Interviews
- Hired Candidates

---

## Company Profile

Fields

- Company Name
- Industry
- Company Logo
- About Company
- Website
- Social Links
- Verification Status

---

## Internship Management

Actions

- Create Internship
- Edit Internship
- Pause Internship
- Close Internship
- Delete Internship

---

## Candidate Management

Actions

- View Candidate
- Shortlist Candidate
- Reject Candidate
- Message Candidate
- Schedule Interview

---

## AI Matching

AI will:

- Score candidates
- Recommend candidates
- Explain match reasons

Match Score

0 - 100

Stored In

applications.ai_match_score

---

## Employer Permissions

Allowed

- Manage internships
- Review candidates
- Schedule interviews

Restricted

- Access admin area
- Manage subscriptions of other employers
